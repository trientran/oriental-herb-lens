# Google Play listing — Med Herb Lens 2.0

## Short description (80 characters max)

Identify medicinal herbs on your device and train your own image AI.

## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Med Herb Lens helps you identify and learn about medicinal plants, with a focus on herbs used in
Vietnam. Identification runs entirely on your device, so it works without an internet connection,
and your camera images never leave your phone.

IDENTIFY
• Point the camera at a plant, or pick photos from your library
• Choose Whole view, or Pick a plant when several plants are in the frame
• See the likely species with how confident the model is

BROWSE 4,799 SPECIES
• Search by scientific, English or Vietnamese name
• Photos for each species from GBIF and iNaturalist, with their authors and licences
• A map of where photos were taken: tap a pin to see them
• Full classification and details from GBIF
• Save favourites to find them again

TRAIN YOUR OWN AI
Train makes your own image classification model from your own photos, on your device. It isn't only
for herbs: use it in any field where photos can tell things apart, such as agriculture and farming,
forestry, biosecurity (spotting pests, diseases or weeds), or teaching and research in medicine.
Share a model with everyone, or try models others have shared.

LEARN AS YOU GO
Tap ? next to any AI term (model, confidence, training, and more) for a plain-language explanation.

CONTRIBUTE
Sign in to add your own plant photos, suggest common names in English or Vietnamese, and share
models. Photos are checked on the device before upload, and anything shared can be reported.

FOR RESEARCH
Med Herb Lens is a research project at the University of New England, Australia. The species list
and GBIF image data are available as an open dataset, and Profile shows how to cite the app.

IMPORTANT
Med Herb Lens is a research preview, and its results are often wrong. Treat a result as a lead to
check, not an answer. Never eat a plant or use it as medicine because of what the app says; ask a
qualified expert first.
