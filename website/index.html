Med Herb Lens

Identify medicinal herbs with your camera

Point your phone at a plant, or pick a photo, and Med Herb Lens suggests which species it may be. Browse thousands of species with their Vietnamese and English names, save your favourites, and share photos to help others and the research behind the app. Identification works on your device, even offline.

Use it in your browser Get it on Google Play Download on the App Store

Med Herb Lens gives no medical advice: never use a plant because of what the app shows.
