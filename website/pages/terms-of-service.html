1. What the App is, and is not

The App suggests which plant species a photo may show and lets you browse a catalog of species, with names and classification. It is an educational and research tool.

The App gives no medical advice. Identifications can be wrong, and a plant can look like a poisonous one. Never eat, drink, apply or use a plant as a remedy because of what the App shows. Ask a qualified health professional and a botanist first.

Common names in the App come from GBIF and from users, and experts have not checked them: a name may be regional, rarely used or wrong. The scientific name is the one that identifies a species.

Models you train in the App, for herbs or anything else, are for learning and research. Don't rely on them alone for decisions that need an expert, such as a diagnosis, whether food is safe, or a biosecurity decision.
